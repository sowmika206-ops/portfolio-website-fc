The project explores the launch of a vibrant clothing collection inspired by traditional textiles, expressive prints and effortless silhouettes.

Through an integrated marketing communication strategy, the campaign develops a cohesive brand experience across digital, physical and experiential platforms. From pre-launch teasers and interactive pop-ups to influencer collaborations, PR kits and post-launch engagement, the project explores how storytelling, visual identity and creative marketing can build anticipation, strengthen audience connections and bring a fashion collection to life.

Campaign Strategy · Creative Direction · Brand Communication · Experiential Marketing

 

Key Deliverables: Pre-launch Campaigns · Pop-up Experience · Influencer Collaborations · PR Kit · Launch Event · Post-launch Campaigns
