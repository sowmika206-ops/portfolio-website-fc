Focusing on the transformation of discarded fabrics into meaningful, functional pieces, the zine highlights the beauty of Kalamkari on Khadi and the cultural stories embedded within handcrafted textiles. Through editorial layouts, textile-inspired graphics, photography and experimental printmaking, the project brings together craft, culture and visual storytelling while exploring how traditional materials can be reimagined for modern fashion.
